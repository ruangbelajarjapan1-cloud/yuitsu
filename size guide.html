Size guide

From the shoulder seam to the hem, measured at the back.

Flat width of the body at chest level. Double it for the circumference.
袖丈Sodetake · Sleeve length
From the shoulder seam to the cuff.
肩幅Kata-haba · Shoulder width
Between the two shoulder seams.
袖口Sodeguchi · Cuff opening
Width of the sleeve opening.
素材Sozai · Fabric
Kimono silk, cotton or other; see each product page.
