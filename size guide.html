Size guide

身丈Mitake · Body length
Shoulder seam to hem, along the back.
裄Yuki · Back neck to wrist
From the center-back neck bone along the arm to the wrist.
袖丈Sodetake · Sleeve length
Length of the sleeve. Standard is about 49 cm.
前幅Maehaba · Front panel width
Width of the front panel at hip level.
後幅Ushirohaba · Back panel width
Width of the back panel at hip level.
衿Eri · Collar
Collar style and width vary by type.
